# PM — app-launch-readiness
Scope and phases: see `.10x/specs/2026-10-03-app-launch-readiness-design.md`.

## User stories (v1)
- As a new user, I install, onboard and make a correct capture with no API key or account.
- I never lose something I said, even offline, interrupted, or when sorting fails.
- When Jot creates a dated item, it tells me exactly what it made and lets me undo.
- I can add a follow-up to something I captured earlier (James's note).
- I can delete and recover from mistakes without hunting for buttons.

## Pricing (proposed, validate in TestFlight)
Free: on-device sorting. Pro: $3.99/mo or $29.99/yr — Claude sorting, unlimited.

## Success
≥30 external TestFlight testers; day-7 retention ≥20% before building payments in earnest.
